Overview

A longer motorcycle journey across four states of Northeast India

This route is designed as a broad overland introduction to Northeast India, linking the river valleys of Assam with the hill roads, frontier regions and cultural landscapes of Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya and Nagaland.

It is a tour for riders who want variety: fast plains, winding mountain roads, remote valleys, villages, monasteries, forests, rivers and highland country, all stitched together by local knowledge and flexible support.

The final route can be shaped around time, season and rider experience, with the emphasis on meaningful travel rather than simply covering distance.

Tour Highlights

  • Four-state motorcycle route across Northeast India
  • Assam river valleys and tea country
  • Arunachal Himalayan roads and frontier landscapes
  • Meghalaya hill roads, waterfalls and villages
  • Nagaland highlands and cultural stops
  • Flexible overland support throughout

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

Western Arunachal Pradesh

Ride into the Monyul region, with mountain roads, monasteries, forested valleys and high Himalayan landscapes shaping the opening section.

Assam and Brahmaputra Valley

Drop into the Brahmaputra Valley, moving through tea country, river roads, wildlife landscapes and the main east-west artery of the region.

Nagaland

Climb back into the hills for highland roads, village landscapes, cultural stops and a very different rhythm of travel.

Meghalaya

Finish through plateau roads, waterfalls, ridgelines, limestone country and village routes before looping back towards Assam.
