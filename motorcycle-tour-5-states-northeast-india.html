Overview

A long motorcycle journey through the hills and valleys south of the Brahmaputra

This route explores a different arc of Northeast India, travelling across Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram and Nagaland. It is built around hill roads, river valleys, borderland cultures, forested ridges and the layered histories of the region south of the Brahmaputra.

Expect variety every few days: the Brahmaputra Valley, Meghalaya's plateau country, Tripura's royal and forest landscapes, Mizoram's long hill roads and Nagaland's highland villages.

The tour is designed for riders who want time on the road, but also time to pause, meet people, eat locally and understand how dramatically the landscape and culture shifts across this compact part of the subcontinent.

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

Assam and the Brahmaputra Valley

Start in Assam, using the river valley as the entry point into the southern and eastern hill states.

Meghalaya

Ride through plateau roads, waterfalls, limestone landscapes and village routes before dropping towards the plains.

Tripura

Continue into a quieter corner of the Northeast, with forest roads, lakes, old royal sites and a softer rhythm of travel.

Mizoram

Move along long hill roads, ridgelines and village landscapes through one of India's most distinctive mountain states.

Nagaland

Finish through highland roads, cultural stops and hill settlements before looping back towards Assam.
