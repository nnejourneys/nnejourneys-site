Overview

An active family adventure through Assam's wildlife, rivers and tea country

This journey takes the spirit of our Assam multi-activity tour and shapes it around families, children and multi-generation groups.

Days can include cycling, short walks, boat rides, wildlife safaris, village visits, local food and easy outdoor adventures.

It works well for families who want variety, comfort and a lively introduction to the Brahmaputra Valley without feeling rushed.

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

North bank

Begin along the quieter northern side of the Brahmaputra, with river landscapes, village roads, forest edges and active days that still work well for families.

Central Assam

Move through Assam's wildlife, tea and river country, combining safaris, short walks, boat rides, local food and easy cultural experiences.

Upper Assam

Continue east into older tea country and culturally rich Upper Assam, with time for cycling, village visits, river landscapes and family-friendly outdoor moments.
