Overview

A flexible and relaxed active holiday in Assam.

This tour combines Assam's wildlife, river life, tea country and culture into one flexible active journey.

Depending on the group, the route can include cycling, walking, boat rides, safaris, village visits and food experiences.

It works well for travellers who want variety and a broad introduction to the Brahmaputra Valley.

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

North bank

Begin along the quieter northern side of the Brahmaputra, with river landscapes, village roads, forest edges and relaxed active days shaped around the group.

Central Assam

Move through Assam's wildlife, tea and river island country, combining safaris, short walks, boat rides, local food and cultural experiences.

Upper Assam

Continue east into older tea country and culturally rich Upper Assam, with time for gentle cycling, village visits, food experiences and river landscapes.
