Overview

A flexible and relaxed active holiday in Assam

This multi-activity tour of Assam brings together the wildlife, rivers, tea country and diverse cultures of the Brahmaputra Valley in one flexible active holiday. It offers an engaging way to experience the region beyond conventional sightseeing.

Depending on the interests and abilities of the group, the itinerary can include cycling along quiet country roads, guided walks, boat journeys, wildlife safaris, village visits and local food experiences. Travellers can explore tea gardens, river islands, forests and rural communities while discovering the landscapes and traditions that shape life along the Brahmaputra.

Activities, daily distances and accommodation can be adapted to create either a relaxed or more adventurous journey. Ideal for travellers who enjoy variety, this Assam multi-activity holiday combines nature, culture and outdoor exploration, offering a broad and immersive introduction to Northeast India.

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

North bank

Begin along the quieter northern side of the Brahmaputra, with river landscapes, village roads, forest edges and relaxed active days shaped around the group.

Central Assam

Move through Assam's wildlife, tea and river island country, combining safaris, short walks, boat rides, local food and cultural experiences.

Upper Assam

Continue east into older tea country and culturally rich Upper Assam, with time for gentle cycling, village visits, food experiences and river landscapes.
