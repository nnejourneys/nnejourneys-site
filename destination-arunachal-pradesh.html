Arunachal Pradesh Tours

Himalayan valleys, monasteries, tribal villages, forest roads, high passes and remote routes across India’s far eastern frontier.

Why Arunachal

India's far eastern frontier of mountains, rivers and living cultures

Arunachal Pradesh tours open up one of the least travelled parts of the Indian Himalayas, from the Buddhist highlands of the west to rainforest valleys and river systems in the east.

Our Arunachal Pradesh holidays are active and deeply place-led: cycling high roads to Tawang, walking through eastern valleys, trekking rainforest trails, exploring the Mishmi Hills, or building family-paced routes around villages, rivers and forest country.

It suits travellers who want remoteness, strong landscapes and genuine time with the cultures of the region, without turning the journey into a race.
