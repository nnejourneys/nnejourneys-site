Overview

An active fixed departure through Eastern Arunachal's river and forest country

This 7-day departure brings together short hikes, cycling, rafting and cultural time in one of the most rewarding corners of Arunachal Pradesh.

The journey is designed for travellers who want variety without rushing: river valleys, forest trails, village roads, local food and time on the water.

It keeps the tone active but accessible, with support and pacing shaped around the group.

Tour Highlights

  • Hike through forest and riverside trails
  • Cycle quiet village and valley roads
  • Raft on Himalayan-fed rivers
  • Explore Eastern Arunachal cultures
  • Travel through remote frontier landscapes

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around local conditions, water levels and the group profile.

Hiking in the Kamlang Wildlife Sanctuary

Begin with forest walks around Kamlang Wildlife Sanctuary, where the route introduces Eastern Arunachal through rainforest, riverside trails and quiet wild country.

Cycling from the Lohit to the Dibang rivers

Ride between two great river systems, moving through valley roads, small settlements and changing landscapes between the Lohit and Dibang.

Rafting in the Dibang

Close the journey with time on the Dibang, rafting through Himalayan-fed waters with the pace shaped around season and river conditions.
