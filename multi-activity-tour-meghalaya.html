Overview

A varied active journey through Meghalaya's hill country

This tour is for travellers who want Meghalaya in motion: walking village trails, cycling quiet hill roads, exploring waterfalls and caves, and spending time in places where culture and landscape are closely woven together.

The route can be shaped around the group, with easier days for relaxed travellers or more active sections for those who want a stronger outdoor rhythm.

Expect a mix of forests, limestone country, living root bridges, local food, viewpoints, markets and community-run stays, with the pace kept flexible and unhurried.

Tour Highlights

  • Walks to living root bridges
  • Short cycling routes on hill roads
  • Waterfalls, caves and viewpoints
  • Khasi and Jaintia village life
  • Local food, markets and culture
  • Flexible multi-activity pacing

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

Cycling

Ride quiet hill roads, village lanes and open plateau sections, with distances adjusted to the group and support close at hand.

Hiking

Walk through forest trails, waterfall country and living root bridge landscapes, moving at a pace that leaves time to look around.

Caving

Explore Meghalaya's limestone country, from easy cave visits to more active underground sections depending on the group profile.

Culture

Spend time with Khasi and Jaintia village life, local food, markets, community traditions and the everyday rhythms of the hills.
