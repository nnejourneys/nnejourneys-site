Overview

A deeper culture tour through the eastern Northeast

Tribes of the East is a Northeast India culture tour built around the people, food, craft, stories and everyday life of the region’s eastern states. It sits well alongside our broader multi-state Northeast India journeys.

The route moves from hill communities and village landscapes into Upper Assam and the frontier regions of Arunachal Pradesh, with time to slow down, listen and understand the cultural range of the region. If you want a more focused state journey, compare it with Arunachal Unravelled or People of the Brahmaputra Valley.

This is a flexible journey, shaped around festivals, local access, village stays, market days and the pace of the group.

Tour Highlights

  • Nagaland village culture and food traditions
  • Upper Assam’s older cultural landscapes
  • Eastern Arunachal’s frontier communities
  • Markets, crafts and local kitchens
  • Flexible route from 12 to 18 days
  • Designed for slow, respectful cultural travel

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, festivals, access, local conditions and group pace.

Meghalaya

Begin with hill country, market towns and communities where landscape, food and daily life are closely woven together.

Nagaland

Move into village landscapes, food traditions, craft, stories and the strong cultural identities of Nagaland’s highlands.

Upper Assam

Spend time in tea country and the wider Brahmaputra Valley, where older Assamese, Tai-Ahom and riverine cultures shape the route.

Eastern Arunachal Pradesh

Finish in the frontier regions of eastern Arunachal, meeting communities shaped by river valleys, forest landscapes and long migration histories.
