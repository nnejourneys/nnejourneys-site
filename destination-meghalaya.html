Meghalaya Tours

Cloud forests, living root bridges, limestone caves, waterfalls, village walks and quiet roads across the Khasi and Jaintia hills.

Why Meghalaya

A hill country shaped by rain, rivers and stone

Meghalaya tours open up one of Northeast India's most atmospheric landscapes: high plateaus, deep valleys, forest trails, living root bridges, cave systems, village paths and waterfalls that shift with the season.

Our holidays keep you active without rushing the experience. Cycle quiet hill roads, walk between Khasi and Jaintia villages, explore caves and river pools, spend unhurried time with local families, or combine Meghalaya with Assam and Nagaland for a wider Northeast India journey.

It is ideal for travellers who want nature and culture together: dramatic scenery, local food, market towns, music, craft and community life, with enough flexibility to make the journey feel personal.

Good for

  • Cycling holidays
  • Walks and treks
  • Family journeys
  • Caves, waterfalls and rivers
  • Living root bridge routes
  • Multi-state extensions to Assam and Nagaland
