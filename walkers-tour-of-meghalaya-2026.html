Overview

A fixed departure through Meghalaya's forests, villages and living bridges

This 7-day walking journey explores Meghalaya at a pace that keeps the landscape close: forest trails, waterfall country, village paths, living root bridges and local food along the way.

The tour is active without being rushed, built for travellers who enjoy meaningful walking days and time to understand the Khasi hills beyond the usual viewpoint stops.

Expect stone steps, river crossings, forest shade, village conversations and the atmospheric green country that makes Meghalaya one of the Northeast's most rewarding walking destinations.

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route can shift slightly with local conditions, trail access and group pace.

Village walks and waterfall country

Begin with Meghalaya's highland routes, village paths and waterfall landscapes, easing into the rhythm of the Khasi hills.

Living root bridge country

Walk into forested valleys shaped by rain, stone steps, streams and living root bridges, with time in communities connected closely to the forest.

Highland viewpoints and local life

Round out the journey with plateau walks, local markets, food, open views and a slower look at life in Meghalaya beyond the headline sights.
