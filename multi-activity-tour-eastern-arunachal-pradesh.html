Overview

A flexible active journey through Eastern Arunachal's river and forest country

This tour is for travellers who want a varied journey through Eastern Arunachal: cycling quiet roads, walking forest trails, rafting clear rivers and spending time with local cultures at the edge of the eastern Himalayas.

The route can be shaped around the group, with gentler days for relaxed travellers or more active sections for those who want a stronger outdoor rhythm.

Expect a mix of river valleys, forests, village roads, local food, tribal cultures and wide frontier landscapes, with the pace kept flexible and unhurried.

Tour Highlights

  • Rafting and river time
