Overview

Trek through one of India's great rainforests

The Rainforest Trek explores Namdapha National Park, a biodiverse rainforest landscape in eastern Arunachal Pradesh.

The route is immersive and nature-led, with forest trails, river crossings and rich bird and wildlife possibilities.

It is a strong choice for travellers drawn to forests, ecology and remote protected areas.

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

Under the canopy

Walk into Namdapha's dense rainforest, where the route moves through humid forest trails, giant trees, layered vegetation and the sounds of one of India's richest protected landscapes.

Grasslands and riverine areas

Explore the park's open grasslands, river edges and floodplain habitats, slowing down for birds, wildlife signs, forest ecology and the changing character of the Noa-Dihing river country.
