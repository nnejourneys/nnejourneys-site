Overview

A family adventure in India's far eastern frontier

This active family holiday introduces families to the rivers, forests and living cultures of eastern Arunachal Pradesh. Designed for families with older children and multi-generational groups, the journey combines outdoor adventure with meaningful encounters in one of Northeast India’s most remote regions.

Days can include rafting on scenic rivers, cycling along quiet rural roads and easy day walks through forests, villages and river valleys. Local encounters offer an opportunity to learn about the region’s diverse tribal cultures, traditional ways of life and close relationship with the surrounding landscape.

Activities and daily distances can be adapted to the ages, interests and comfort of the group. With a relaxed pace and plenty of time for discovery, this eastern Arunachal Pradesh family tour balances cycling, rafting, walking and cultural exploration—creating an active yet comfortable introduction to India’s eastern Himalayan frontier.

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

Walks in the rainforest

Begin with gentle forest trails, river edges and time under the canopy in one of the richest rainforest landscapes of the Northeast.

Culture in the Theravada belt

Spend time in the Buddhist cultural belt of eastern Arunachal, with monasteries, villages, food and local traditions woven into the journey.

Raft on the Dibang

Add a soft adventure day on the Dibang, using the river as a playful way to experience the scale and beauty of the valley.

Activities in the Mishmi Hills

Round off the journey with short walks, viewpoints, village time and nature-based activities in the Mishmi Hills.
