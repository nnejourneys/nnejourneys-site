How to Choose Between Assam, Meghalaya and Arunachal Pradesh

Pick the state that best matches your pace, interests and comfort level

Overview

These three states suit very different kinds of travellers

If you are planning a first trip to Northeast India, Assam, Meghalaya and Arunachal Pradesh are often the three most useful starting points. They connect well, but they do not offer the same experience.

Assam is usually the easiest for variety and comfort. Meghalaya works beautifully for walking, hill landscapes and shorter active routes. Arunachal Pradesh is usually the choice for travellers who want stronger remoteness, bigger landscapes and a more demanding journey.

The best choice depends on whether you care most about culture, walking, cycling, family travel, remoteness or overall ease.

Quick fit

  • Assam: easiest all-round introduction
  • Meghalaya: best for walking and hill landscapes
  • Arunachal Pradesh: best for remoteness and scale
  • Family travel: often Assam or Meghalaya first
  • Harder cycling: often Arunachal Pradesh

State by state

What each one does best

Assam

Assam is usually the most forgiving choice for first-time visitors who want a bit of everything: wildlife, tea country, river landscapes, culture and gentler active days. It works especially well for family holidays, easier cycling trips and broader cultural routes such as People of the Brahmaputra Valley.

Meghalaya

Meghalaya is ideal if you are drawn to green hills, root bridges, village walks, waterfalls and shorter active journeys. It feels compact and atmospheric, and it is often the best state for travellers who want walking without stepping into a full expedition model. Start with Walkers Tour of Meghalaya or our guide to walking holidays in Northeast India.

Arunachal Pradesh

Arunachal Pradesh is for travellers who want stronger remoteness, mountain scale, more complex logistics and a deeper frontier feeling. It is the most expansive of the three, and often the most demanding. Pages like Road to Tawang, Arunachal Unravelled and our permits guide help show what planning there involves.

How to decide

Choose by travel style, not just by map

Choose Assam if you want the smoothest entry into the region, especially for mixed-interest travel, wildlife, tea country and lower logistics pressure.

Choose Meghalaya if you want walking, waterfalls, village life and a greener, hill-based journey that still feels comfortable and accessible.

Choose Arunachal Pradesh if you want the strongest sense of remoteness, bigger mountain landscapes, monasteries, tribal cultures and routes that feel more exploratory.

If you are unsure, season can help decide too. Pair this page with our guide to the best time to visit Northeast India.
