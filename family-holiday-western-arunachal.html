Overview

A family-friendly window into western Arunachal's mountains and monasteries

This family route explores the Buddhist belt of western Arunachal at a comfortable pace.

It combines scenic drives, short walks, village time, monasteries and mountain landscapes.

The route is designed to give families the feeling of the Himalayas without making the trip too demanding.

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

Wildlife parks at the foothills

Begin along the Himalayan foothills, with time for Assam's forests, river country and wildlife reserves before the road climbs into Arunachal.

Culture, cuisine and monasteries

Move through western Arunachal's villages and Buddhist landscapes, with monasteries, local food, short walks and easy family-paced cultural time.

The high Himalayas along the Tibetan border

Continue into the higher country toward Tawang, where mountain roads, wide valleys, prayer flags and borderland landscapes shape the final section of the journey.
