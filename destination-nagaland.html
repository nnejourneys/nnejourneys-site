Nagaland

Hill roads, village culture, food, music, craft, festival season and routes linking Meghalaya to Kohima.

Why Nagaland

A highland culture with a strong sense of place

Nagaland brings together hill landscapes, village life, music, craft, food and a cultural identity that feels distinct even within the wider Northeast.

Our Nagaland journeys are built around active routes into and around the state, especially during the Hornbill Festival season. Cycling offers a slower, more connected way to enter the cultural heart of the state, while motorcycle and culture tours cover greater distances.

It works especially well as part of a wider Northeast India journey, linking naturally with Meghalaya, Assam and longer multi-state routes.

Good for

  • Cycling routes to Kohima
  • Hornbill Festival season
  • Village culture
  • Food, music and craft
  • Hill roads and highland landscapes
  • Multi-state journeys with Meghalaya and Assam
