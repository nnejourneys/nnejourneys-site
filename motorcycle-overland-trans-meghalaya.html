Overview

A cross-Meghalaya road journey built around backroads, hill country and local life

This motorcycle and overland tour moves across Meghalaya through a mix of plateau roads, forested descents, village routes, riverside halts and borderland landscapes.

The route is designed for travellers who want the freedom of the road without rushing through the state. Days combine riding or driving with short walks, waterfalls, caves, viewpoints, local food and time in Khasi, Jaintia and Garo hill country.

It can be run as a motorcycle journey, an overland 4x4-style holiday, or a mixed format depending on the group.

Tour Highlights

  • Motorcycle and overland routes across Meghalaya
  • Khasi, Jaintia and Garo hill country
  • Waterfalls, caves and river valleys
  • Village roads and lesser-used backroads
  • Local food, markets and cultural stops

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

Garo Hills and western approaches

Begin with the quieter western side of Meghalaya, riding or driving through forested roads, small settlements and hill country that sees fewer visitors.

Khasi ridgelines and waterfall country

Move through highland roads, viewpoints, river valleys, waterfalls and village routes, with time for short walks and local food along the way.

Jaintia backroads and border landscapes

Continue east through limestone country, caves, clear rivers and borderland roads before looping back towards Guwahati.
