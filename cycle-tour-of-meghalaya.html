Overview

Ride the cloud-catching hills of Meghalaya

This cycling tour explores the dramatic hill country of Meghalaya—a land shaped by monsoon clouds, deep-green valleys, limestone caves, cascading waterfalls and distinctive village cultures. Winding mountain roads and quiet rural trails make Meghalaya one of Northeast India’s most scenic and rewarding cycling destinations.

The route can focus on the Khasi and Jaintia Hills or extend westwards into the remote landscapes of the Garo Hills. Along the way, riders pass through villages, lively local markets, forested valleys and rolling agricultural country.

Expect challenging climbs, exhilarating descents and visits to Meghalaya’s remarkable living root bridges. Encounters with Khasi, Jaintia and Garo communities introduce riders to the region’s matrilineal societies, local food traditions and deep connections with the surrounding landscape.

From high ridges overlooking the plains of Bangladesh to mist-covered forests and hidden waterfalls, a cycling tour in Meghalaya combines adventurous riding with nature, culture and unforgettable mountain scenery.

Journey Flow

The tour in sections

This is an indicative flow. The final route is shaped around your dates, pace, group profile and local conditions.

The Jaintia Plateau

Begin on the high plateau of eastern Meghalaya, riding through rolling hill roads, limestone country, small settlements and open ridgelines that reveal the scale of the landscape.

The Bangladesh border

Drop toward the southern edge of Meghalaya, where the hills fall sharply toward the plains of Bangladesh. This section brings dramatic viewpoints, humid valleys, waterfalls and a clear shift in terrain.

Root bridges and whistling villages

Move through forested villages, living root bridge country and communities known for their distinctive traditions, including whistling villages. The riding slows into a deeper encounter with Meghalaya's people, forests and paths.
