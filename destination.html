The region

Where the Himalayas turn east

The Brahmaputra and Barak valleys sit at the heart of the region, framed by the Eastern Himalayas to the north, the Patkai and Naga ranges to the east, and the Khasi, Jaintia and Garo Hills to the south.

The people

Many cultures, languages and ways of life

The Northeast is one of the most culturally and linguistically diverse regions of the Indian subcontinent. Its communities form living bridges between India, Southeast Asia, Inner Asia and the eastern Himalayas.

The environment

Rainforests, rivers and biodiversity hotspots

High rainfall, steep relief and close proximity to the Himalayas have created a remarkable range of ecosystems, from alpine zones and cloud forests to wetlands, grasslands and tropical rainforests.
