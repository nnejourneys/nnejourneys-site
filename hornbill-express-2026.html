Overview

Ride from the Khasi Hills to the Naga highlands

The Hornbill Express links Shillong and Kohima through a cross-border cycling route timed around the Hornbill Festival season.

The journey combines hill roads, village landscapes, cultural encounters and the celebratory energy of Nagaland.

It is a fixed-departure adventure for riders who want a strong cultural finish to an active route.

Tour Highlights
