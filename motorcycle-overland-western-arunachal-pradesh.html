Overview

A high-road motorcycle journey into the Monyul region of Western Arunachal Pradesh.

This motorcycle and overland route travels from the Assam foothills into Western Arunachal Pradesh, a region of high passes, deep valleys, monasteries and Himalayan roads.

The Monyul is home to communities such as the Monpa, Sherdukpen and Brokpa, with cultural and religious links stretching towards Bhutan and Tibet. The route reflects that geography: from the warm river plains to forests, ridges and high mountain country.

Riding conditions change with altitude, weather and road work. Assam's flatter highways gradually give way to winding hill roads, broken sections and jeepable tracks closer to the high country.
